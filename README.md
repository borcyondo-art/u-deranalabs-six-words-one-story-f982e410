## The story

> She outlived three husbands. Still knitting.

Three lifetimes of marriage, outlived — and the ordinary thing that
goes on. How they died, her grief, and whom the knitting is for are
left for the reader to fill in.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node, standard library only, no network) writes the line
above — exactly, with no trailing newline — to `/output/entry.txt`,
deterministically, on every run:

```sh
node main.ts
```

The output is 44 characters and exactly 6 words, inside the gate of
1–60 characters of UTF-8 text.
